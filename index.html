The Story
Why this video exists

Every agency knows the gap. You build a campaign page, then you spend the next hour setting up tracking so you can actually measure it. Tag Manager snippets. UTM strings. Event configurations. Then you refresh Google Analytics and pray the data shows up.

Havola eliminates that gap entirely. When you build a campaign asset on Havola — a site, a link, a QR code — the analytics are already running. There's nothing to configure. The platform built the asset AND the tracking layer, so the data flows from the first visit.

The video shows this loop in real time: build → publish → live data → drill down → client report. The product is on screen for 70% of the video because the product is the proof.
